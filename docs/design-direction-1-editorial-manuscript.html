01 / Core Ontology

The Tender as Primary Entity

Every surface in APT-LABS derives from a single domain object: the Tender. Not a listing, not a project \u2014 a tender: a scoped, accountable build with a verifiable bill of materials, a three-person crew, and a capital target that must settle before material release.

The ontology is deliberately narrow. Six categories. Three crew roles. Eight lifecycle states. One immutable Roll. This constraint is the design \u2014 it makes every interface decision traceable to a domain rule.

APT-LABS connects local demand, capital and execution through open Tenders. The market compares an imported alternative with a proposed local bill of materials; completed builds enter the public Roll only after an assigned Anchor signs the active Blueprint.
\u2014 README.md, Product Manifesto
Domain invariants (enforced in tender.ts & SQL):
  • Exactly one Anchor, Builder, Apprentice per Tender
  • Blueprint line totals must equal assetBuildCostMinor
  • Illustrative tenders cannot carry verified funding
  • Roll records only reference deployed, non-illustrative tenders
  • School Console records never leave the device

04 / Crew & The Roll

Three Roles. One Signature. Immutable Proof.

The Roll is not a log \u2014 it is a provenance ledger. A record enters only when: (1) Tender is deployed, (2) non-illustrative, (3) Anchor signs the active Blueprint hash, (4) all blueprint lines match. The database function deploy_tender() enforces this atomically.

05 / Hard Boundaries

What This System Does Not Do

The design must make these limits visible, not hidden:

  • No payment processing \u2014 Funding is contact-intent only until an approved payment/compliance design exists
  • No connected AI / OCR \u2014 Auto-Marking is deterministic keyword matching on teacher-supplied CSV/JSON
  • No message delivery \u2014 Communication links require explicit user click; no delivery claim
  • No encrypted multi-user school storage \u2014 School Console is local-first IndexedDB only
  • No funder accounts, bidding, escrow, yield, SBT issuance \u2014 The cockpit shows these limits instead of simulated balances
The checked-in market examples are explicitly illustrative. Without Supabase configuration, the public market uses those local examples and the Roll is empty. No sample funding or completion figure represents money received or work delivered.
