# Funder: The Roddenberry Foundation — Catalyst

- **Eligibility/status:** Rapid early-stage catalytic support is programme-specific. Confirm the current intake, decision criteria, and whether the proposed work is eligible.
- **Leading APT-LABS face:** The Roll + one bounded prototype
- **Secondary face:** APT Deploy or APT Fab, depending on the prototype
- **Evidence required:** A small, concrete, demonstrable intervention; a sharp question; a credible test; and a visible evidence artefact.
- **Current evidence available:** Prototype architecture, hash/seal machinery, evidence states, and an intentionally empty asset register.
- **Missing evidence:** Selected prototype, budget, test plan, prototype evidence, local operator, and completion criteria.
- **Claims prohibited:** A finished infrastructure system, verified cost savings, beneficiaries, partnerships, or impact before the bounded test.
- **Relevant route(s):** `/deploy`, `/fab`, `/roll`, `/roll/assets`, `/evidence`
- **Application thesis:** Use a small grant to test whether a documented, sealed asset record and local-first institutional prototype can make one technical intervention more reproducible and maintainable.
