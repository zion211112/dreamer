The Call

Every Tender begins with a summons \u2014 a Principal names the work, the place, and the need.

The Call is not a form submission. It is a deliberate act: a Principal or Tender Issuer from a school or community organization declares an asset that must be built. The Call carries weight \u2014 it commits the issuer to scoping a Blueprint, assembling a three-person Crew, and standing for the result.

I name this work. I define the asset, the place, and the measure of its completion. I accept responsibility for the Blueprint\u2019s truth.

No Tender exists without a Call. No Call proceeds without an organization, a category, and a location. The ontology enforces this at the database level \u2014 organizations, tender_category, and location are all required, non-nullable fields.

The Assembly

Three distinct persons. Three distinct roles. One shared Blueprint.

The Assembly is the moment the Crew is constituted. Exactly one Anchor, one Builder, one Apprentice \u2014 enforced by unique indexes on crew_members. Each role carries specific authority and no more:

\u263E
Anchor

Signs the Blueprint hash. Authorizes deployment to the Roll. Holds final accountability.

\u2692
Builder

Advances through Materials and Fabrication. Receives, logs, constructs. Reports evidence.

\u270D
Apprentice

Observes, documents, contributes capability evidence. Learns the craft of accountable building.

The Assembly is recorded in crew_members with show_publicly defaulting to false. Names enter the public record only with explicit consent. The database enforces: one active Anchor per Tender, no duplicate user-role pairs.

The Capital Rite

Settled capital must reach the target before materials release. No simulation. No optimism.

The transition open \u2192 materials is the only gate the system enforces automatically. tenderLifecycle() in tender.ts computes settlementReached as currentSettledAmountMinor \u2265 fundingTargetMinor. Until this is true, permitted is empty. The Builder cannot advance. The Anchor cannot sign. The work waits.

Capital is not a number. It is a covenant between funders and the Crew. Each allocation carries basis: 'illustrative' | 'verified'. Illustrative scenarios cannot carry verified funding. The Roll records only verified settlements.

The Sign-Off

The Anchor signs the active Blueprint hash. The work becomes immutable.

The Sign-Off is a cryptographic ceremony. The Anchor signs:

  • crewMemberId \u2014 their identity
  • blueprintId \u2014 the specific Blueprint version
  • blueprintHash \u2014 the content hash of that Blueprint
  • signedAt \u2014 the timestamp

This record lives in anchor_sign_offs with a unique constraint on (tender_id, blueprint_id). The deploy_tender() database function verifies the signature matches the active Blueprint before allowing deployment. No signature, no Roll entry. No hash match, no Roll entry.

I have seen the work. The artifact matches the Blueprint. The evidence is sufficient. I affix my seal.

The Roll

Immutable provenance. Six fields. No edits. No deletions.

When deploy_tender() succeeds, a RollRecord is created atomically. It captures:

FieldMeaningSource
contributionWho did what (role, hours, scope)Crew attestation
artifactWhat was produced (source, version, hash)Builder delivery
testMethod, conditions, result, limitationsBuilder evidence
acceptanceWho accepted/rejected/deferred \u2014 and whyAnchor judgement
maintenanceFailure, repair, operating state, next reviewOngoing stewardship
integritySubmitted evidence distinct from reviewedSystem guarantee

Illustrative tenders never enter the Roll. The migration 20261019050000 enforces this via is_illustrative checks. The Roll is the only surface where verified funding appears without qualification.

The Return

The cycle completes. The asset serves. The Crew disperses. The knowledge remains.

The Return is not an end \u2014 it is the moment the deployed asset enters service and the Crew\u2019s work becomes part of the collective record. The School Console on the Second Floor operates on the same principle: local records, local accountability, no external dependency.

The work speaks. The Roll does not advocate. It testifies. Future builders read the evidence and decide what to trust.

Four floors. One frame. The lift moves between them, but the ceremony is the same: Call \u2192 Assembly \u2192 Capital \u2192 Sign-Off \u2192 Roll \u2192 Return.
