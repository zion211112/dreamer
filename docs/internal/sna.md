## I. THE ROLL: FROM ASSET LEDGER TO SOVEREIGN IDENTITY

The Roll today is a local-first, SHA-256-sealed register of people and assets.
It is a prototype, not a shared database. That is honest.

The Roll *becomes* the cryptographic heart of the SNA. It transitions into the
registry for **Soulbound Contribution Tokens (SBTs)** and the execution
environment for **Zero-Knowledge Sortition.**

### Sweat-Equity Minting

In legacy systems, power is bought. In APT-LABS, governance weight is
**minted through verifiable physical and digital labor.**

Citizens earn SBTs on The Roll not by buying them, but by:
- maintaining an APT Fab microgrid (physical labor, logged to the register)
- contributing code to APT Studio (digital labor, hashed to the roll)
- successfully arbitrating a local supply-chain dispute (institutional labor,
  attested by a BenBen build record)

The SBT is soulbound: non-transferable, non-divisible, bound to the physical
identity that earned it. No speculation. No whale accumulation. No exit.

### ZK-Sortition Oracles

When a local decision must be made — where to deploy the next water
purification node, which crop to prioritize in a closed-loop farm — The Roll
runs **ZK-Sortition**: a random selection algorithm that picks proven
contributors to form a temporary, anonymous jury.

The ZK layer ensures:
- **Anonymity:** jury members cannot be identified or coerced after selection.
- **Proof of contribution:** only contributors with verifiable SBTs are
  eligible. No Sybil participation.
- **Ephemerality:** the jury dissolves after the decision. No permanent
  power bloc forms.

### Decoupling Identity from State

The national ID system in most post-colonial states is a weapon. It is
tribal-coded, politically gated, and fragile. The Roll's cryptographic
identity layer is **locally hosted, self-sovereign, and offline-resilient.**

A citizen's identity in the SNA is not a state-issued document.
It is a hash that the community sealed, witnessed by an APT Studio or
APT Fab node, and stored on the local roll.

This is the first true decoupling: the individual's claim to
participate in public-goods allocation no longer depends on
a state that may exclude them.

## II. APT FAB: THE KINETIC DEPLOYMENT ARM OF QUADRATIC FUNDING

Cryptographic voting is useless if it does not manifest in the physical world.
APT Fab is the **output layer** of the network's Quadratic Funding (QF) pools.

### Algorithmic Resource Allocation

Rather than bidding on government contracts — which are fraught with
kickbacks, political capture, and corruption — APT Fab executes projects
funded **directly by the community** through the BenBen Builds treasury
interface.

Citizens allocate stablecoins to proposed infrastructure:
- mesh Wi-Fi towers
- solar clinics
- water purification nodes
- local fabrication equipment

The QF protocol **mathematically matches** these contributions.
The threshold is set by the community. Once it is met, APT Fab
deploys the hardware. No middleman. No procurement officer. No
kickback.

### Radical Transparency

The entire Bill of Materials, labor costs, and maintenance schedule
are **published back to The Roll** the moment deployment is complete.
This is not optional. It is the protocol.

A community that cannot see what was built, what it cost, and who
maintains it, is not sovereign. It is a client.

### Anti-Plutocracy Engineering

Because QF favors **broad community support** over whale donors,
APT Fab is mathematically forced to build what the majority actually needs.
This completely bypasses local political patronage networks.

The richest person in a village cannot outvote the poorest
if the QF pool is weighted by *number of contributors*,
not by *size of contribution*.

This is the first structural anti-corruption measure that
does not require a court, an audit, or a government.

Without them, the "Meat-Space Coercion Vector" — where local warlords
or corrupt officials physically threaten citizens to control their
ZKPs — becomes the dominant failure mode.

APT Studio nodes serve as **decentralized digital sanctuaries.**

### Air-Gapped Governance

Each Studio node provides:
- **Encrypted, high-throughput compute** for generating complex
  Zero-Knowledge Proofs locally.
- **Air-gapped operation** where the network demands it — no
  reliance on state-controlled telco infrastructure.
- **Offline resilience** as a first-class design constraint, not
  a fallback.

A community that cannot prove a ZK offline, in the face of a
disrupted telco or a coercive official, does not have sovereign
governance. It has a fragile dependency dressed up as a protocol.

### Skill Verification

APT Studio is also the **educational and verification hub** where
citizens master the skills required to mint higher-tier SBTs:
- agritech
- node operation
- basic cryptography
- local fabrication

This is not optional. The digital divide is not bridged by
giving a poor person a phone. It is bridged by giving them
**the capacity to operate the protocol that governs their life.**

## V. DEFENDING THE PROTOCOL: FAILURE-MODE MITIGATION

To prevent the architecture from becoming a **digitized version of
post-colonial extraction**, the following protocols are non-negotiable.

### 1. Sybil & Foreign Capture Defense

The Roll must require **in-person, localized verification** for
initial onboarding. A user cannot mint a baseline SBT without a
**physical cryptographic handshake** at an APT Studio or APT Fab site.

This neutralizes:
- bot networks
- foreign proxy accounts
- Sybil attacks by any actor who cannot physically
  stand in the room where the hash is sealed

The physical handshake is the anchor. Without it, the protocol
is just a website. With it, the protocol is **sovereignty.**

### 2. The Falsifier Check: Capture Dashboard

BenBen Builds must feature a **publicly visible "Capture Dashboard."**

If the data reveals that the **top 10% of SBT holders** are
dictating **more than 40% of the QF infrastructure deployment**,
the protocol automatically triggers a **"Governance Slashing"**
event: voting weights are temporarily flattened until
decentralized equilibrium is restored.

This is the protocol's immune system. Without it, the
top 10% become the new patronage class. With it, the
protocol self-corrects.
