# ADR B4 — Visual design direction (Structural Emergence)

**Status:** Active  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Scope:** Visual system (tokens, typography, layout, devices)

## Decision

Active design language: **Structural Emergence** — warm ivory base, earthy neutrals, restrained sage, deep forest/ink, selective lilac/peach accents. Editorial serif for record names; precise operational sans/mono for interface and readouts.

## Six emergence operations

1. Indeterminacy — clean, spacious field.
2. Differentiation — clear boundaries and hierarchy.
3. Polarity — meaningful relationships exposed.
4. Recursion — feedback, iteration, self-reference.
5. Identity — persistent, coherent structures.
6. Emergence — whole system greater than individual components.

## Hard anti-patterns (no exception)

- Generic SaaS dashboards
- Endless card grids
- Decorative gradients / glows
- Unnecessary glassmorphism
- Arbitrary rounded corners
- Fake spatial depth
- Unexplained visual metaphors
- Excessive animation
- Decorative graphs without operational meaning
- Beautiful screens with incomplete functionality

## Quality standard

Every visual decision must have a structural or experiential reason. Every interaction must have a clear consequence. Every complex workflow must have an understandable entry point, progression, feedback, and completion state.
