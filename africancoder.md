# KILO — Pro Website Build System

## Product brief

APT-LABS is a company and portfolio of independent sovereign infrastructure products. The School Console is one product. Tender System and other products belong to the wider portfolio; do not collapse the company into one application.

Build a website, not a generated collection of pages. Treat the site as a visual argument: every element must establish what APT-LABS is, what it builds, what matters, what a visitor can do, or what evidence supports a claim.

## Visual thesis

An austere infrastructure company from 2060: physical systems, cryptographic state, evidence, contribution, allocation, and human judgment made legible without looking like a generic AI dashboard.

## Art direction

- Near-black graphite, precise hairline geometry, restrained typography, and deliberate negative space.
- Teal/cyan for verified or live state; violet for intelligence; amber for uncertainty and physical execution.
- Use color as state, never as decoration. Avoid excessive glow, glass, gradients, cards, and generic AI motifs.
- Product design should feel distinct from company-level identity. The console is a product, not the company website.
- Favor editorial composition, topology, technical diagrams, and product evidence over rows of interchangeable cards.

## Product and evidence rules

- Present APT-LABS as a multi-product company. Do not make the School Console the definition of the company.
- Name Tender System as a portfolio product, but do not invent its features, implementation status, customers, or outcomes.
- Separate product identity from product maturity. Label unknown details as unknown; do not imply deployment or adoption.
- Keep architecture, physical infrastructure, evidence, contribution, allocation, and human judgment visible as the shared thesis.
- Every control must have a real behavior; every claim must have a defensible state.

## Craft standard

Use the existing Next.js, React, TypeScript, and CSS system. Inspect the running result at desktop and mobile sizes. Check hierarchy, keyboard focus, contrast, responsive behavior, and real interactions before calling the work done. Prefer removing weak elements over adding decoration.
