# Deep Research Plan: African Liberation Infrastructure

## Research Areas (10+ sites each)

### 1. Top African Tech Companies & Unicorns
- Flutterwave, Andela, Chipper Cash, Wave, M-Pesa ecosystem
- Jumia, Takealot, Kobo360, Lori Systems
- African Development Bank, AfCFTA digital trade

### 2. Anti-Extraction / Post-Extractive Economics
- Circular economy in Africa
- Resource nationalism movements
- Community ownership models
- African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) protocols

### 3. Alternative Governance & Democracy Models in Africa
- Participatory budgeting (Kenya, South Africa)
- Blockchain governance experiments
- Customary law integration
- Decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs) in Africa

### 4. African Developer Ecosystems / "African GitHub"
- Andela, Decagon, ALX, Moringa School
- GitHub Africa programs
- African open source communities (AfriLabs, iHub, CcHub)
- Code for Africa, Ushahidi

### 5. Research Infrastructure in Africa
- African Academy of Sciences
- ARUA (African Research Universities Alliance)
- Open access initiatives (African Journals Online, SciELO SA)
- Pan-African research networks

### 6. Labor Markets & Tender Systems in Africa
- Government procurement portals (Kenya IFMIS, South Africa eTenders)
- Gig economy platforms (Kubwana, Lynk, Gigs Africa)
- Construction/tender platforms

### 7. Sovereign Infrastructure / Digital Public Goods
- Digital Public Goods Alliance
- MOSIP (Modular Open Source Identity Platform)
- OpenG2P, GovStack
- African Union Digital Transformation Strategy

### 8. Cryptographic/Blockchain for Governance in Africa
- Cardano Africa (Ethiopia, Tanzania)
- Celo, Kotani Pay
- Bitcoin adoption (CAR, Nigeria P2P)
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (eNaira, Sand Dollar)

### 9. Anti-Colonial / Decolonial Tech Movements
- Data sovereignty movements
- Indigenous knowledge systems in tech
- African language NLP (Masakhane, Lanfrica)

### 10. Climate/Physical Infrastructure in Africa
- Desert to Power, Great Green Wall
- Mini-grid / off-grid energy
- Water infrastructure innovation
