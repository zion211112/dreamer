# Funder: Grand Challenges Africa / Science for Africa Foundation

- **Eligibility/status:** Call-specific. The current/recent AI call is not a generic education or hardware call; verify the open call and its exact scientific and development question before considering an application.
- **Leading APT-LABS face:** None activated by default; potentially APT Deploy, APT Studio, or The Roll only if a call maps directly to its evidence and hypothesis.
- **Secondary face:** Depends on the call; do not default to a broad infrastructure pitch.
- **Evidence required:** Call-specific scientific hypothesis, measurable developmental outcome, cohort or population, research design, ethics/safeguarding, and dissemination plan.
- **Current evidence available:** Technical prototypes and evidence states; no call-matched scientific study or health/education outcome dataset.
- **Missing evidence:** Matching open call, formal research question, validated population, outcome instrument, ethics approval where required, and study data.
- **Claims prohibited:** “Pan-African innovation” as generic fit, health impact, education impact, or scientific validation without a call-specific design.
- **Relevant route(s):** `/deploy`, `/studio`, `/roll`, `/evidence`
- **Application thesis:** No generic thesis. Build a call-specific thesis only when one open call names a problem that APT-LABS can test with an appropriate research design.
