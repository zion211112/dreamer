# Funder: USAID Development Innovation Ventures (DIV)

- **Eligibility/status:** Stage 1 is a call-specific early-innovation pathway. Confirm the current solicitation, geography, and Stage 1 requirements before submission.
- **Leading APT-LABS face:** APT Deploy + APT Fab
- **Secondary face:** The Roll
- **Evidence required:** Real-world demand, feasibility, a bounded pilot, comparative cost or operating evidence, local production logic, and a credible measurement plan.
- **Current evidence available:** Offline software prototype, documented state model, planned fabrication pathway, and an evidence register.
- **Missing evidence:** Verified demand interviews, unit economics, local fabrication record, comparative baseline, pilot data, and adoption evidence.
- **Claims prohibited:** Imported-system superiority, verified cost savings, fabricated capacity, or scale readiness before measurement.
- **Relevant route(s):** `/deploy`, `/fab`, `/roll`, `/evidence`
- **Application thesis:** Test whether a locally owned and documented Deploy + Fab architecture can reduce institutional technology dependence under connectivity, cost, and supply-chain constraints, with cost and performance measured against an explicit baseline.
