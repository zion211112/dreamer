# APT-LABS · Plain Ontology

## The Seven Things

| Entity | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Node** | A physical place — a school, a workshop, a studio. Has people, tools, and a ledger. |
| **Person** | A human with a role: operator, anchor, builder, or learner. Owns their own record. |
| **Build** | A physical object or media piece. Has a blueprint, a cost, and named makers. |
| **Blueprint** | Open documentation of how to make a build. Forkable by any node. |
| **Record** | Proof of who made what. Owned by the maker, not by any school or employer. |
| **Treasury** | Money that flows back from operating savings. Funds the next build. |
| **Ledger** | The public, append-only record of everything above. |

---

## The Rules

1. **Free tools open the gate.** Every node enters through a zero-cost utility that solves a real daily problem.
2. **No build without an anchor.** A verified tradesperson must sign off on physical fabrication before materials are released.
3. **Every blueprint is open.** The second node pays nothing for the first node's learning.
4. **Every record is owned by its maker.** It survives school, employer, and state.
5. **Null results are published.** A failed claim on a public ledger is still worth the cost of the attempt.
6. **Local spend is reported.** Line by line. Every grant dollar's path is visible.

---

## The Four Roles

| Role | What they do | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator** | Runs the node. Signs off on treasury flows. | Stewardship, not ownership. |
| **Anchor** | Master tradesperson. Verifies physical builds. Signs records. | A share of build revenue. Standing in the network. |
| **Builder** | Designs, fabricates, or animates. | A record. A cut of the savings their build generates. |
| **Learner** | Apprentices under a builder or anchor. | Skill, a record, and a path to becoming either. |

---

## The Four Evidence States

Every claim in the system is one of:

- **Built** — deployed, working, verified on-site.
- **Prototype** — exists in physical form, not yet at scale.
- **Planned** — design exists, no physical proof yet.
- **Unknown** — claim exists, no evidence either way.

No other states. No inflated adjectives. If it's not Built, it says so.

---

## The Loop

```
Intent ──▶ Detection ──▶ Assembly ──▶ Build ──▶ Record ──▶ Savings ──▶ Treasury ──▶ Intent
   ▲                                                                                    │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Intent** — a community says what it needs.
**Detection** — the free tool surfaces the people who can build it.
**Assembly** — an anchor, a builder, and a learner form a crew.
**Build** — the physical thing or media piece is made.
**Record** — the makers are credited on the public ledger.
**Savings** — the build saves the host institution money.
**Treasury** — savings flow back. The next build is funded.

---

## What It Is Not

- Not a software company. Software is the door.
- Not a charity. It runs on savings, not donations.
- Not a political movement. It builds physical things.
- Not a credential mill. Records come from work, not exams.
- Not anti-import. It's pro-repair, pro-design, pro-ownership.

## Operating Rules

1. **Free tools open the gate** — Every node initializes via zero-cost utility solving an immediate, daily friction point.
2. **No build without an anchor** — Physical fabrication requires sign-off from a verified master tradesperson before material release.
3. **Every blueprint is open** — Secondary nodes replicate previous learning at zero marginal cost.
4. **Every record belongs to the maker** — Credentialing survives state, institutional, and employer changes.
5. **Null results are published** — Failed attempts are logged transparently to the public ledger.
6. **Local spend is reported** — Every grant dollar's path is tracked line by line.

## Evidence States

Every claim maps strictly to one of four states:

* **Built** — Deployed, operational, and verified on-site.
* **Prototype** — Physically realized but pre-scale.
* **Planned** — Documented in design; unbuilt.
* **Unknown** — Unverified claim with zero evidentiary proof.
