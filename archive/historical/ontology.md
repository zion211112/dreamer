# APT-LABS — Ultimate Ontology and Concept

**Status:** Foundation synthesis (2026-11-10).
**Scope:** Conceptual files only. No code changes.
**Relation to charter:** This is the single coherent ontology that the APT-LABS Constitution (`docs/design/charter.md`, Layer A) and System Reference (`docs/APT-LABS-SYSTEM.md`) are built upon. Where this document and the charter overlap, the charter governs.

---

## 1. Purpose

APT-LABS is infrastructure for making fragmented productive capacity visible, connectable, governable, locally retained, and cumulative. It does not sell software; it makes the relationships between people, crews, institutions, assets, skills, needs, opportunities, work, and evidence legible and actionable so that real economic activity can be discovered, organised, evidenced, and repeatedly activated.

This document synthesises the ultimate ontology and concept: the smallest set of underlying primitives capable of explaining the entire system, expressed so that the core is stable and the boundaries are open-ended.

---

## 2. The Core Ontology

APT-LABS is built on one shared economic fabric. Every product, sector, and workflow expresses the same six underlying structures; none is forced into a single rigid role.

| Structure | What it holds | Examples |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| **Actors** | People, crews, enterprises, institutions | A mason, a fabrication crew, a school, a hospital |
| **Resources** | Skills, assets, capital, knowledge | Welding skill, a micro-hydro rig, savings, a blueprint |
| **Intentions** | Needs, opportunities, proposals | "We need a workshop bench", "An animation asset for investment" |
| **Commitments** | Agreements, roles, obligations, authority | A tender, a crew role, a payment term, an authorisation |
| **Activity** | Events, work, exchange, delivery | Pouring concrete, rendering a frame, signing off a build |
| **Knowledge** | Evidence, credentials, outcomes, lessons | A Roll entry, an SBT attestation, a dispute record |

### Canonical entity distinctions (never silently equated)

- **Person ≠ Asset:** a person is an individual with agency, not inventory.
- **Person ≠ Crew:** a crew performs work; a person is a member.
- **Institution ≠ Venture:** an institution commissions or authorises work; a venture is a bounded undertaking.
- **Asset ≠ Capability:** an asset is a thing; a capability is the ability to use it.
- **Claim ≠ Evidence:** an assertion is bounded by subject, predicate, and scope; evidence substantiates it.
- **Approval ≠ Authorization ≠ Execution ≠ Acceptance ≠ Settlement:** each is a distinct, separately timed flow.
- **AI suggestion ≠ institutional decision:** the system proposes; people decide.

These distinctions are the invariants. Everything else is domain expression.

## 3. The Economic Progression

Every completed cycle follows the same underlying movement:

**Reality → structured records → relationships → coordinated work → verified outcomes → reusable intelligence → economic value**

- **Reality:** a real need, a real asset, a real person doing real work.
- **Structured records:** the need, the asset, the person are typed, attributable, time-aware, and correctable.
- **Relationships:** who can do what, for whom, under what authority.
- **Coordinated work:** resources, agreements, and participants brought together toward an outcome.
- **Verified outcomes:** evidence attached to the work, accepted or rejected on defined criteria.
- **Reusable intelligence:** what worked, what failed, what to reuse — preserved as institutional memory.
- **Economic value:** access to demand, contracts, services, capital, and new opportunities.

This progression is not a mandatory linear workflow. Steps can repeat, branch, be skipped, or terminate without success.

## 4. Value Architecture

APT-LABS creates four kinds of value and must be honest about which it captures, through what mechanism, and why participants keep paying.

| Value | What APT-LABS creates |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Productive value** | More useful work completed with existing and newly developed capacity. |
| **Coordination value** | Search, negotiation, assembly, execution, and verification become less costly. |
| **Institutional value** | Evidence, operational knowledge, and relationships accumulate and remain usable. |
| **Economic value** | Participants gain access to demand, contracts, services, capital, and new opportunities. |

**Value creation ≠ value distribution ≠ value capture.**
- **Creation:** the network produces more useful work at lower coordination cost.
- **Distribution:** who benefits — makers, institutions, coordinators, learners.
- **Capture:** which part APT-LABS keeps, via institutional subscriptions, operational infrastructure fees, coordination fees, verification services, integrations, or specialist deployment services.

Capture mechanisms are hypotheses to test, not revenue streams to declare simultaneously. Creating enormous value while capturing very little is possible; extracting too much can destroy the network.

**Network size is not network density.** Five hundred participants who repeatedly cooperate to deliver real economic outcomes are more valuable than ten thousand registered accounts. The objective is useful production coordinated per unit of coordination cost.

## 5. Coherence: One Underlying System

The deeper primitive is the **undertaking**: a bounded attempt to organise resources, authority, obligations, and participants toward an intended outcome.

A tender is one way to formalise demand. So are a proposal, an agreement, an institutional initiative, or a community-led effort. Not everything must become a tender.

Anchor, Builder, Apprentice are **possible roles**, not universal classes. A person anchors one undertaking and apprentices in another.

Coherence comes from the shared economic fabric (Section 2), not from identical workflows. Different products and sectors express the same structures without being forced into identical forms. This is how coherence and expansion coexist.

## 6. Recursion: Each Cycle Makes the Next More Capable

APT-LABS must not merely complete work and store its history. Each completed cycle should improve the conditions under which future work becomes possible:

1. **Discover** capacity and demand — real participants, needs, resources, opportunities.
2. **Compose** cooperation — the right capabilities, agreements, and resources.
3. **Execute** production — actual work and economic exchange.
4. **Capture evidence** — contributions, costs, outcomes, failures, obligations.
5. **Create recognition** — attestations and institutional memory.
6. **Improve future coordination** — subsequent discovery, assessment, and cooperation more effective.

**The next cycle starts with more capability than the last.**

Compounding effects:
- More completed work → better evidence of actual capability.
- Better evidence → reduced uncertainty for future collaborators.
- More reliable relationships → lower coordination costs.
- Better operational records → improved planning and allocation.
- More participants → opportunities for complementary production.
- More reusable designs and methods → lower cost of future deployments.

Recursion also works when things fail: rejected work, failed experiments, disputes, and cancellations improve future decisions if recorded properly.

**This is not an automatic network effect.** More users can mean more noise, duplicated supply, disputes, and administrative cost. The loops that produce measurable compounding must be distinguished from those that are merely conceptual.

## 7. Factory Readiness: A Production Grammar

A factory is a repeatable system for turning inputs into valuable outputs under controlled conditions. APT-LABS uses a general production grammar — reusable, not a mandatory linear workflow:

| # | Stage | What happens |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 01 | **Intake** | Receive and structure demand |
| 02 | **Diagnose** | Understand the actual problem |
| 03 | **Compose** | Design the solution and resources |
| 04 | **Authorise** | Establish authority and commitments |
| 05 | **Produce** | Coordinate and execute work |
| 06 | **Inspect** | Test against defined requirements |
| 07 | **Settle** | Resolve obligations and exchange |
| 08 | **Learn** | Preserve evidence and improve |

Activities can repeat, branch, be skipped, or terminate without success. The same grammar works across physical, digital, creative, institutional, and service production.

**Factory readiness demands:**
- Standard inputs and output specifications.
- Reusable templates and procedures.
- Responsibility and authority.
- Quality and acceptance criteria.
- Resource and cost accounting.
- Exception and failure handling.
- Evidence and auditability.
- Maintenance and support.
- Replication and improvement.

The software factory and APT-LABS's own physical production (APT Fab, APT Studio) share the same grammar but are not the same business operation.

## 8. Openness and Generativity

APT-LABS must support industries, institutions, instruments, geographies, and organisational forms its designers have not yet anticipated.

### Five forms of openness

| Openness | Architectural requirement |
|----------|---------------------------|
| **Ontological** | New entity types, records, relationships, and events can be introduced. |
| **Operational** | New production methods and workflows can be defined. |
| **Economic** | New contractual, financial, and exchange instruments integrate without rewriting the core. |
| **Institutional** | New governance arrangements, authorities, and organisational forms can participate. |
| **Technical** | External software, APIs, identity systems, data sources, and infrastructure interoperate. |

This is not an ungoverned free-for-all. It is a **stable core with extensible boundaries**:

- **Core (stable invariants):** shared identity, provenance, relationships, time, authority, evidence, commitments, events, and the canonical entity distinctions.
- **Extensions (domain boundaries):** domain-specific meanings and operations for agriculture, fabrication, healthcare, creative production, education, etc.

The charter protects the invariants; it does not freeze the current implementation.

### Generativity

APT-LABS must be able to produce new capabilities, products, workflows, institutions, and economic relationships from its existing foundations. That is stronger than extensibility (developers adding features); generativity allows participants to create new forms of productive organisation.

## 9. Modules and Lenses

One workspace, one shared graph, five modules:

| Module | Job | Route |
|--------|-----|-------|
| **Commons** | Discover and express needs, offers, resources, opportunities, knowledge, problems | `/workspace/commons` |
| **Network** | Understand and connect people, crews, institutions, assets, capabilities, relationships | `/workspace/network/people`, `/workspace/network/institutions`, `/workspace/network/capabilities` |
| **Workbench** | Scope, assemble, coordinate, manage productive work | `/workspace/workbench` |
| **Records** | Inspect durable history, provenance, evidence, credentials, attestations | `/workspace/records/{canon,annals,roll}` |
| **Intelligence** | Agents, graph analysis, matching, forecasting, analytics | `/workspace/intelligence` |

**School Console** is a nested operational boundary under Workbench (a workplace where people take jobs), not a primary module.

**Canon / Annals / Roll** are three lenses within Records, not independent modules:
- **Canon** — what is structurally recorded: entities, identities, typed relationships, accepted state.
- **Annals** — what happened over time: chronological events, changes, decisions, transitions.
- **Roll** — why a claim or outcome can be substantiated: evidence, provenance, attestations, verification.

## 10. Two Levels of Governance

**Level I — The enduring constitution** (docs/design/charter.md, Layer A)

Defines why APT-LABS exists, the economic transformation it seeks, its fundamental principles, its common ontology, its trust/evidence/authority principles, its recursion and value-creation logic, its participant rights and governance, its openness and generativity, and its obligations to preserve local productive capacity and fair value distribution.

**Level II — The evolving operating doctrine**

Defines current product architecture, commercial hypotheses, production systems, economic instruments, technical standards, industry extensions, deployment and scale gates, current priorities and experiments.

This separation prevents a constitution so vague it guides nothing, and one so rigid it prevents evolution.

## 11. Boundary Conditions: What APT-LABS Is Not

- Not a social network; conventional job board; generic marketplace; collection of institutional dashboards; blockchain or SBT product; generic project-management application; or AI chatbot with business-themed screens. Those may describe isolated features. None describes the core.
- Not a charity: sustained by operational savings, not donations.
- Not a credential mill: records derive from labor, not exams.
- Not a sovereign tender engine that forces every activity into a tender. A tender is one way to structure an undertaking, not the only one.
- Not a universal reputation score that collapses different forms of trust into one number.
- Not on-chain identity or payment as a prerequisite for participating in productive work.
- Not fake functionality, invented data presented as real, or demo disclaimer theater.
