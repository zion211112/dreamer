Ontology in this view

Claim ≠ Evidence: the assertion is bounded by subject, predicate, and scope; each artifact is linked as evidence explaining how and within what scope it supports the claim.

Origin, claim basis, verification method, lifecycle, and disclosure are separate. A record-level badge is not universal evidence of truth.
