Six structures. None stands alone.

Every product, sector, and workflow in APT Labs expresses the same six underlying structures. They overlap and interpenetrate.

Actors People, crews, enterprises, institutions with persistent identity A mason · A fabrication crew · A school
Resources Skills, assets, capital, knowledge that actors steward or operate Welding skill · A micro-hydro rig · A blueprint
Intentions Proposed actions and recorded needs that initiate work "We need a workshop bench"
Commitments Undertakings and packages that bind actors to scope and acceptance A tender · A crew role · A payment term
Activity Recorded outcomes, evidence, and hash-anchored roll entries Pouring concrete · Signing off a build
Knowledge Findings, attestations, and instruments that institutions can act on A Roll entry · An SBT attestation

The problem is fragmentation, not absence.

African communities are not empty environments waiting for intelligence to be introduced. They already contain it — practical knowledge, informal coordination, local adaptation, institutional memory. APT Labs makes that intelligence legible, cumulative, and actionable.
