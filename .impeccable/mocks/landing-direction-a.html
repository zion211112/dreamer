The ontology

Six structures. None stands alone.

Every product, sector, and workflow in APT Labs expresses the same six underlying structures.

01

Actors

People, crews, enterprises, institutions. A mason. A fabrication crew. A school.

02

Resources

Skills, assets, capital, knowledge. Welding skill. A micro-hydro rig. A blueprint.

03

Intentions

Needs, opportunities, proposals. "We need a workshop bench."

04

Commitments

Agreements, roles, obligations, authority. A tender. A crew role. A payment term.

05

Activity

Events, work, exchange, delivery. Pouring concrete. Rendering a frame. Signing off.

06

Knowledge

Evidence, credentials, outcomes, lessons. A Roll entry. An SBT attestation. A dispute record.
